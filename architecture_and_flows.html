⚡ The Problem Statement 4 Challenge

Adoption and utilization of renewable energy are increasing and are expected to increase sharply in the future. However, renewable generation is highly variable. Coordination across solar, wind, batteries, and market prices is therefore critical to optimizing cost and sustainability.

The AI agent must autonomously coordinate solar generation, wind generation, battery storage, grid imports, wholesale energy prices, and industrial demand forecasts every 15 minutes to minimize cost, maximize clean energy utilization, and maintain grid reliability.

Physical Portfolio Managed
10 Cyber-Physical Utility Assets
  • 5 Solar Farms (230 MW): Geographically dispersed across microclimates, subject to spatial cloud front attenuation.
  • 3 Wind Farms (250 MW): Coastal and ridge terrain, governed by cut-in (3 m/s) and storm safety cut-out (25 m/s) boundaries.
  • 2 Battery Storage Systems (150 MW / 500 MWh): BESS-01 (400 MWh Bulk LFP) + BESS-02 (100 MWh Fast Peaker LTO).
  • 3 Heavy Industrial Consumers (~155 MW): Baseload, flexible green hydrogen electrolyzer (shiftable), and electric smelter (interruptible DR).
  • 500 kV Regional Interties (200 MW export): Subject to thermal line ratings and transmission congestion limits.
  • Wholesale ISO/LMP Market: Real-time spot trading, forward spreads, negative pricing tariffs, and $35/ton carbon compliance.
